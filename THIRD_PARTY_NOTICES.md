# Third-party interface components

Dashboard layout adapted from [shadcn/ui dashboard-01](https://ui.shadcn.com/blocks). Sidebar, cards, tables, controls and sheets are sourced from the official new-york-v4 registry. Registry imports were adjusted for this Vite application. Sample business content was replaced with CreditGraph data; no sample revenue charts are used.
